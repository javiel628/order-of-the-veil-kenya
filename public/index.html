About us

We strengthen the mind, character and relationships behind meaningful progress.

Order of the Veil Kenya is a community platform for people who value learning, discipline, reflective thinking and responsible leadership. Our focus is conscious personal development, respectful relationships and meaningful collective progress.

We are built around healthy values: accountability, lawful conduct, mutual respect and ethical action. We do not support coercion, intimidation, exploitation or harmful practices. We create a safe and constructive environment for meaningful conversations and personal growth.

What is the purpose of Order of the Veil?

It is a Kenya-based community committed to knowledge, discipline, unity and ethical personal development.

Is this meant to be harmful or coercive?

No. The community is intentionally grounded in lawful, respectful and constructive principles. We do not support harm, exploitation or intimidation.

Who can apply?

Anyone seeking a respectful, values-led community focused on personal development and mutual responsibility may apply.
